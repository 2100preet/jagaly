FAQ

Is it mandatory?+

No. Verified Tenant is optional and free. Most owners accept all tenants; some may choose to accept only verified tenants.

Does Jagaly store my Aadhaar?+

No. Verification is done by a licensed KYC partner. Jagaly keeps only the result (“ID verified”), your name and, if you choose, your employer.

Can I delete my verification?+

Yes, anytime from your account. Consent is recorded and can be withdrawn under the DPDP Act.
