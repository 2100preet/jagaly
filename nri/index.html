Power of Attorney — guidance

  • A specific PoA for one property transaction is usually safer than a general PoA.
  • Executed abroad, a PoA is typically signed before an Indian Embassy / Consulate officer, or notarised and apostilled in Hague Convention countries.
  • Once it reaches India it generally needs to be adjudicated and stamped in Karnataka within the prescribed time, and a PoA used to sell or transfer property is typically registered at the Sub-Registrar’s office.
  • Jagaly NRI would help draft the PoA with partner lawyers and coordinate stamping and registration.

Guidance only, not legal advice. Requirements vary by country and transaction; confirm with a qualified lawyer.

NRE/NRO, FEMA & TDS — the basics

  • FEMA: NRIs can generally buy residential and commercial property in India, but not agricultural land, plantation property or farmhouses (except by inheritance).
  • Paying for it: via inward remittance or from NRE / NRO / FCNR(B) accounts; home loans from Indian lenders are available to NRIs.
  • Rent: usually credited to an NRO account; tenants paying rent to an NRI are generally required to deduct TDS.
  • Selling: the buyer generally deducts TDS on the NRI seller’s capital gains (Section 195). A lower-deduction certificate can be applied for in some cases.
  • Repatriation: sale proceeds may be repatriated subject to limits and conditions (for example, the annual limit on NRO remittances and Form 15CA/15CB requirements).

General information only, not tax or legal advice. Rates, limits and rules change; check current RBI/FEMA and Income Tax rules with a chartered accountant.
