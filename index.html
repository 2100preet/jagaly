For owners

Verification that doesn’t ask for your sale deed

Tier 1 · to list

Phone OTP + a live in-app video walk-through with GPS. That’s all you need to go live.

Tier 2 · optional “Verified Owner”

Upload a recent property tax receipt or electricity bill. Blur everything except name + address in-app. Kept private, used only for a name match, deletable anytime. Ranks higher.

Tier 3 · only at deal stage

When a serious tenant or buyer proceeds, documents are checked — for sales, by partner lawyers.

Optional for tenants

Verified Tenant

Verify your ID once via DigiLocker or Aadhaar offline e-KYC through a licensed partner. Appear first in owners’ inboxes, book visits faster and qualify for deposit-free plans.

Jagaly never stores your Aadhaar number. Owners see only “ID verified”, your name and employer if you share it.
