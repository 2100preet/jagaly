How verification works

Owners never need to upload a sale deed to list. Tier 1 (required): phone OTP plus a continuous walk-through recorded live in the Jagaly app — we check the GPS geotag against the listed locality and run frame-continuity checks, then a human reviewer approves Jagaly Proof. Tier 2 (optional “Verified Owner”): a recent property tax receipt or electricity bill, blurred in-app so only name + address are visible; kept private, used only for a name match, deletable anytime. Tier 3 (deal stage only): when a serious tenant or buyer proceeds, documents are checked — for sales, by partner lawyers. Re-verification every 60 days or after edits.

How we make money

Optional paid services — Tenant Plus, owner Assured plans, Buyer Pro legal checks, optional Deposit Safe add-ons and planned Jagaly NRI services — plus builder project listings that must pass our delay-tracker disclosure. We never sell your phone number. Ever.

FAQ

Do I need to upload a sale deed to list my property?+

No. To list you only need phone OTP + a live in-app video walk-through with GPS (Tier 1). Ownership documents are checked only at deal stage, when a serious tenant or buyer proceeds.

What is the “Verified Owner” badge?+

An optional Tier 2 badge. Upload a recent property tax receipt or electricity bill, use the in-app blur tool so only your name and address are visible, and we match the name to your account. The document is kept private, never shown to tenants or buyers, and you can delete it anytime. Verified Owner listings rank higher.

What happens at deal stage?+

Tier 3: when a tenant or buyer is ready to proceed, ownership documents are checked before money changes hands. For sales, this is done by partner lawyers.

Is Deposit Safe required?+

No. Deposit Safe is optional for owners and tenants. Owners who opt in get a badge and ranking boost; listings without it are never hidden.

Can I list or find commercial property?+

Yes. Offices, co-working, shops, showrooms, F&B spaces, warehouses, industrial sheds, commercial land and residential plots. The same Jagaly Proof tiers apply: phone OTP + live video to list, no sale deed needed. Title, OC/CC and zoning are checked only at deal stage. Commercial listings get True Price per sq ft, a Property Truth Score and a lease cost calculator.

Is Verified Tenant required?+

No. It’s an optional, free ID check through a licensed KYC partner. Owners can choose to accept only Verified Tenants (off by default).
