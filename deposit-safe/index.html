Deposit Safe

Bangalore’s 10-month deposit problem, solved.

Tenants in Bangalore routinely hand over 6–10 months of rent with nothing but trust. Pakka Deposit Safe keeps it in a bank escrow with a regulated partner — Pakka never holds the money — and releases it on a fair, documented checklist.

How it works

Four steps. Zero awkward WhatsApp fights.

1

Sign a digital rental agreement

Standard Karnataka 11-month agreement, pre-filled from the listing. e-Stamp + Aadhaar e-sign in 10 minutes (planned integration).

2

Deposit goes into escrow

You pay the deposit into a dedicated bank escrow account with a regulated partner — not the owner’s savings account. Pakka never holds your money.

3

Move-in condition report

Both sides record a timestamped photo + video checklist of every room on move-in day inside the app.

4

Fair, fast move-out

On move-out, the same checklist is repeated. Undisputed amount is released within 48 h; disputes go to a neutral mediator, decided in 7 days.

Without Deposit Safe

  • Deposit sits in the owner’s account, often spent
  • “Painting charges” of 1–2 months deducted arbitrarily
  • Refund takes weeks of follow-up calls
  • No evidence of move-in condition
  • Only remedy is a slow civil court case

With Deposit Safe

  • Money held in escrow, untouchable by either party
  • Deductions only for documented damage, capped by agreement
  • Undisputed amount released in 48 hours
  • Shared photo/video move-in & move-out reports
  • Neutral mediation in 7 days, binding by agreement

For owners

Guaranteed deposit is real money (not a promise), tenants are KYC-verified, and rent can be auto-collected via UPI AutoPay.

For tenants

Option to pay just 2 months upfront with a Deposit Safe bond covering the rest (planned, via insurance partner).

FAQ

Who holds the money?+

A regulated bank / trustee escrow partner (to be finalised). Pakka never holds your money — not in its own accounts, not on its balance sheet.

Is the digital agreement legally valid?+

Yes — e-stamped agreements with Aadhaar-based e-signatures are commonly used in Karnataka. Registration requirements depend on term; we will guide you. (Not legal advice.)

What does it cost?+

Draft: ₹499 one-time per agreement, split 50/50 by default. See pricing.

What if the owner refuses?+

Deposit Safe-eligible listings are marked. Owners opting in get a badge and rank higher in search.
