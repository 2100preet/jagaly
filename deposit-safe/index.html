FAQ

Do I have to use Deposit Safe to list?+

No. Listing on Jagaly has zero obligation. Deposit Safe is optional for every tenancy; you can opt in when you post, later, or only when a tenant requests it.

What if a tenant requests it and I decline?+

Nothing happens to your listing. You can still rent to that tenant the usual way.

Who holds the money?+

A regulated bank / trustee escrow partner (to be finalised). Jagaly never holds the money.

How are damages decided?+

Both sides record in-app video walk-throughs at move-in and move-out. AI flags differences room by room; documented damage is released to the owner. Anything disputed goes to a neutral mediator.

What does it cost?+

Opting in is free for owners and includes a free digital agreement. Optional protection add-on and deposit-free plan fees are on the pricing page (draft).
