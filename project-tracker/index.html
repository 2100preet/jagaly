Method

  • Sample: Random sample (seed 2026) of 60 of 1992 projects: district=Bengaluru Urban, type=Residential/Group Housing, registration status APPROVED, registered 2018-01-01..2024-12-31.
  • Original completion: the completion date recorded at registration.
  • Current completion: the proposed completion date currently on the project record.
  • Extension months: whole months from original to current completion date. Nothing is estimated; projects without a later date show “No extension in record”.
  • Record status: “Completion applied” if the record shows a completion application date; “Completion date passed · no completion application in record” if the current completion date is before 7 Sept 2026 and no application is shown; otherwise “Within current completion date”. This is a statement about the record, not about construction on site.
  • Complaints listed: the count of complaints against the project shown on the record. A complaint is not a finding.
  • No grades or scores are assigned to promoters.

Not shown / caveats

  • Quarterly progress reports are filed as documents and aren’t available as structured data, so construction progress isn’t shown.
  • Records reflect the portal on 7 Sept 2026; anything filed later (extensions, completion applications, OCs) won’t appear until the next refresh.
  • Five records were spot-checked against the official certificate pages (registration number, approval date and original completion date all matched).
  • Always confirm on rera.karnataka.gov.in before any decision. Corrections: hello@jagaly.example.
